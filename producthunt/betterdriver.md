# BetterDriver — Product Hunt Launch

## Tagline
Open-source navigation app for blind and low-vision drivers

## Description
BetterDriver is a free, open-source navigation app built for blind and low-vision drivers. It delivers turn-by-turn voice guidance, route planning, and accessibility-first design. Android APK available now; iOS and Google Play coming Q1 2027.

## Full Description

**The problem:** Millions of blind and low-vision people worldwide rely on generic navigation apps that weren't designed for them. Voice guidance is an afterthought, not the primary interface. Visual cues dominate, and critical maneuvers (lane changes, exits, roundabouts) are described too late or not at all.

**The solution:** BetterDriver is built from the ground up for auditory navigation. Every interaction is voice-first, every notification is spoken, and the route engine optimizes for clarity — not just distance.

**Key features:**
- **Voice-first navigation:** Every turn, exit, and maneuver announced with precise timing
- **Route planning:** Accessible interface for setting destinations, avoiding tolls, and choosing transport modes
- **Real-time rerouting:** Detours and traffic changes announced immediately
- **Offline support:** Pre-download maps and routes when data is unreliable
- **Fleet management (Q2 2027):** Organizations can manage multiple drivers and routes

**Why it matters:**
- Independence: blind drivers can navigate confidently without sighted assistance
- Safety: proactive alerts reduce missed exits and dangerous maneuvers
- Community-driven: open source means accessibility advocates and developers can improve it together

**Current status:**
- Android APK (74MB) available for download and testing
- Basic navigation and voice guidance fully functional
- Route planning implemented
- Next: Google Play launch, iOS app, and premium features

**Use cases:**

- Route planning for accessibility organizations
- Fleet management for para-transit services

**Our commitment:**
