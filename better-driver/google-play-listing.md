## Full Description

Better Driver is your personal driving assistant, designed to make every trip smoother, safer, and more efficient. Using advanced sensor fusion and on-device intelligence, Better Driver analyzes your driving habits in real time and provides actionable feedback that helps you improve without being intrusive.

**Core Features:**

- **Real-Time Coaching**: Get gentle, timely nudges when you accelerate too hard, brake too sharply, or exceed your preferred speed. Coaching is adaptive—Better Driver learns your style and adjusts suggestions to match your comfort level.

- **Fuel & Energy Efficiency**: Track consumption patterns, identify inefficient driving behaviors, and receive tips that can save you money at the pump or on your electric vehicle charging bill. Average users report up to twelve percent improvement in fuel economy within the first month.

- **Safety Score & Insights**: Better Driver assigns a safety score based on hard accelerations, hard braking, speeding, and phone use while driving. Weekly reports show trends, highlight dangerous patterns, and celebrate progress. Share your score with family or fleet managers if you choose.

- **Route Analysis**: After each trip, review a detailed map of your route with event markers, speed graphs, and efficiency highlights. Identify problem intersections, risky road segments, and optimal departure times.

- **Trip Logging & Export**: Automatic trip detection, categorization, and logging. Export data to CSV or PDF for expense reporting, tax deductions, or fleet management. Integration with popular mileage tracking workflows.

- **Music & Audio Control**: Control music, podcasts, and audiobooks with simple voice commands or gesture controls, keeping your eyes on the road. Better Driver integrates with Android Media Session and major audio apps.

- **Privacy First**: Better Driver runs mostly on your device. Location data, sensor readings, and trip details stay local unless you explicitly opt into cloud backup. No account is required to use core features. We do not sell your driving data.

- **Battery Optimized**: Careful use of GPS, accelerometer, and gyroscope ensures minimal impact on battery life. Background processing is optimized for Doze mode and modern Android power management.

**Why Better Driver?**

Most driving apps are either gamified toys with no real impact or invasive fleet trackers that treat you like a liability. Better Driver is different. It is built for real people who want to become better drivers without sacrificing privacy or autonomy. The interface is calm, the coaching is respectful, and the insights are genuinely useful.

**Who Is It For?**

- Commuters who want to reduce stress and save fuel
- Parents teaching teens to drive safely
- Fleet managers monitoring vehicle usage
- Rideshare and delivery drivers tracking expenses
- Environmentally conscious drivers reducing their carbon footprint
- Anyone who wants to understand and improve their driving habits

**Requirements**

- Android 10.0 or later
- Location and sensor permissions for driving analysis
- Optional: Google Drive backup for trip history
