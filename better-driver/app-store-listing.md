## Description

Better Driver is your intelligent co-pilot, helping you develop safer driving habits, reduce fuel consumption, and stay calm behind the wheel. Built with on-device machine learning and privacy-first design, Better Driver gives you personalized feedback without compromising your data or your attention.

**What Better Driver Does:**

- **Coaches You in Real Time**: Instead of harsh criticism, Better Driver offers gentle, contextual suggestions. Accelerate smoothly, maintain safe following distances, and avoid unnecessary stress—all with subtle cues that help you learn naturally.

- **Measures Your Impact**: Track fuel usage, energy consumption, and trip efficiency over time. Visualize your progress with clean charts and actionable insights. See how small changes in behavior lead to real savings at the gas station or charging station.

- **Keeps You Safe**: Monitor speeding, hard braking, and rapid acceleration. Receive a weekly safety score that highlights areas for improvement and celebrates milestones. Share progress with family members or keep it private—your choice.

- **Maps Every Trip**: After each drive, review a detailed timeline of your route. See where you braked hard, where you saved fuel, and where traffic affected your efficiency. Build a history of your most common routes and identify patterns that help you plan better.

- **Logs for Work and Taxes**: Automatic trip detection categorizes drives as personal, business, or commuting. Export logs to PDF or CSV for expense reports, tax deductions, or reimbursement requests. Seamless integration with accounting workflows.

- **Controls Audio Without Distraction**: Manage music, podcasts, and navigation prompts with voice commands and simple gestures. Better Driver integrates with CarPlay and supports popular audio apps so you can stay focused on the road.

- **Protects Your Privacy**: Better Driver is designed to run locally. Your location history, sensor data, and trip details never leave your device unless you explicitly enable encrypted iCloud backup. No account creation required. No ads. No data sales. No exceptions.

- **Saves Battery**: Optimized background processing respects iOS power management. Better Driver uses location and motion sensors efficiently so you can drive all day without draining your battery.

**Why Drivers Love Better Driver:**

Most driving apps are either overly punitive or irrelevant. Better Driver is supportive. It does not lecture you; it helps you understand your own behavior. The interface is clean, the feedback is timely, and the insights are genuinely useful for everyday drivers, professional fleets, and anyone who wants to reduce their environmental impact.
