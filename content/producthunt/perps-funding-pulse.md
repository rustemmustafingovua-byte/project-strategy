# Perps Funding Pulse — Product Hunt Launch Kit

## Tagline
Real-time perpetual swaps dashboard. Funding rates, OI, CVD. Free, no paywalls.

## Description
Perps Funding Pulse is an open-source analytics dashboard for perpetual swaps. It streams live funding rates, Open Interest, Cumulative Delta Volume (CVD), and orderbook liquidity from Binance, Bybit, and OKX — and it's completely free.

**Key features:**
- Real-time funding rates across top-10 perps pairs
- CVD visualization showing smart money flows
- Open Interest dynamics
- Orderbook depth charts
- Telegram alerts for funding spikes and OI shifts

No "pro tier," no email signup, no $49/month subscription. Just transparent market data.

## Target audience
- Perpetual swaps traders (scalpers, swing traders, position traders)
- Quant researchers looking for free perps data
- Developers who need an open-source market data pipeline
- Anyone tired of paying for data that should be public
