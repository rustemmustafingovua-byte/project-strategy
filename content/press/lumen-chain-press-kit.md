# Lumen Chain — Press Kit

## Facts
- **Name**: Lumen Chain
- **Version**: 0.7.2
- **License**: MIT
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Consensus**: Proof-of-Stake (PoS)
- **Native token**: LMN
- **Supply**: 1,000,000,000 LMN
- **Block time**: ~2 seconds

## Key Features
- TypeScript-native blockchain
- Built-in AMM
- x402 payment protocol
- SQLite persistence
- P2P gossip network
- Autonomous keeper
- Staking, farming, governance
