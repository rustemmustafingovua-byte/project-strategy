## Title
Lumen Chain: DeFi & Blockchain Explorer

## Subtitle
Track assets, monitor wallets, and explore the decentralized web.

## Description

Lumen Chain is the all-in-one blockchain companion for anyone who wants to understand and interact with decentralized finance without the complexity. Whether you are tracking a portfolio across multiple chains, monitoring wallet activity, or learning how blockchains work, Lumen Chain brings the entire ecosystem to your pocket.

**Key Features:**

- **Multi-Chain Portfolio Tracker**: Connect wallets from Ethereum, Solana, Cosmos, and more. View balances, token values, and allocation charts in real time without switching between apps.

- **Transaction History & Analytics**: Search any address or transaction hash and get instant details. Track gas usage, block confirmations, and smart contract interactions with a clean, filterable timeline.

- **x402 Payment Support**: Pay for APIs, content, and services directly from the app using the x402 standard. No more copying addresses or juggling multiple wallets for microtransactions.

- **Wallet Hygiene Scanner**: Review connected wallets for risky approvals, unused permissions, and legacy contracts. Protect your assets before interacting with new protocols.

- **Educational Modules**: Learn blockchain fundamentals, DeFi concepts, and security best practices through interactive lessons. Perfect for beginners and experienced users alike.

- **Secure Local Storage**: Private keys and seed phrases never leave your device. All sensitive data is encrypted with device-level security and biometric authentication.

- **Cross-Chain Bridge Tracker**: Monitor bridge transactions, estimate arrival times, and receive alerts when assets cross between networks.

**Why Lumen Chain?**

Most blockchain apps force you to choose between simplicity and power. Lumen Chain gives you both. The interface is clean enough for a first-time user, but the underlying data and tools are deep enough for a professional trader, protocol researcher, or DeFi developer. We built Lumen Chain because we were tired of switching between five different apps to do one thing: understand what is happening on-chain.

**Privacy & Security**

Your data is yours. Lumen Chain does not sell analytics, does not track you across apps, and does not require account creation to use core features. All blockchain data is fetched directly from public nodes and indexed sources. No middlemen, no ads, no surveillance.

**Who Is Lumen Chain For?**

- DeFi users managing portfolios across multiple chains
- Developers debugging transactions and contract interactions
- Analysts tracking whale movements and market trends
- Newcomers who want to learn blockchain safely
- Protocol teams monitoring contract health and user behavior

**Requirements**

- iOS 16.0 or later
- Internet connection for blockchain data
- Optional: biometric authentication for enhanced security
